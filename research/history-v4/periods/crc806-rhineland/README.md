# Rhineland early Neolithic settlement-density research areas

Hilpert, Meesenburg, Wendt, Zimmermann (2019), Rhine-LUCIFS / CRC806, University of Cologne. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Source: https://crc806db.uni-koeln.de/dataset/show/distribution-maps-of-early-neolithic-in-central-europe-and-in-rhineland1559904469/

Source period: 7500–6950 cal BP (before AD 1950). These are public generalized high-settlement-density areas aggregated over that period, not annual maps, political borders or evidence that every site existed in each included year. Terraevia transcribed original WGS84 Shapefile rings without changing coordinates, order or holes and added source/integrity metadata. No restricted site locations were downloaded or published. Original archive, metadata and licence evidence are retained in source/. Historical accuracy has not been independently certified.
